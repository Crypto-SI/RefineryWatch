Privacy Policy

Privacy-safe by design

We aim to use aggregated analytics and collect only the information needed to run newsletters, contact forms, and site operations.

Data we may collect

Email, country, investor type, contact messages, aggregated page views, country-level traffic, newsletter signups, and broker directory click totals.

Data we do not display

We do not publish IP addresses, exact user locations, individual visitor journeys, or real-time personal data on the Live Interest Map.

Analytics
